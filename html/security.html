FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does CCTV installation cost in Melbourne?

Pricing depends on your site, so we confirm it after an assessment. The main factors are the number of cameras, the camera type, the recorder and storage, and how difficult the cable runs are. You receive a written quote before any work starts.

How long does an installation take?

Most home systems are installed in a single day. Larger commercial systems can take longer, and we will confirm the timeframe in your quote.

How many cameras do I need?

Most homes start with 4 to 6 cameras covering the front door, driveway, side access and backyard. Businesses usually need coverage of every entrance, high-value areas and car parks. We recommend positions during the site assessment so you are not paying for cameras you do not need.

Can you install cameras at an existing home?

Yes. Most of our residential installs are at established homes. We plan cable routes through the roof space, walls or external conduit to keep the installation neat with minimal disruption.

Do you offer on-site quotes?

Yes. For most jobs we visit the property to check camera positions, cabling and internet before giving a written quote. Simple jobs can sometimes be quoted from photos and a phone call.

Do you service my suburb?

We work across Melbourne, with a focus on the western suburbs including Werribee, Hoppers Crossing, Point Cook, Tarneit and Truganina. If you are unsure, send a quote request with your suburb and we will confirm.

Can I watch my cameras on my phone?

Yes. We set up the camera app on your phone, tablet or computer so you can watch live, play back recordings and receive motion alerts. A stable internet connection at the property is needed for remote viewing.

Can you upgrade my existing CCTV system?

Yes. We can replace old cameras or recorders, add cameras, increase storage or move from an analogue system to IP cameras. Where the existing cabling is in good condition we can often reuse it.

Can you connect CCTV with an alarm or intercom?

Yes. We can install CCTV, alarms, video intercoms and access control together so they work from the same app where the equipment supports it, or add them to a system you already have.

What warranty do I get?

Cameras and recorders are covered by the manufacturer's warranty, and our workmanship warranty terms are included in every quote. If something stops working, contact us and we will help.
