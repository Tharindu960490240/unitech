FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you work as a subcontractor for carriers and head contractors?

Yes. Much of our work is delivered for carriers and head contractors on fibre, NBN and network projects. Contact us with the scope and we will confirm crew availability.

Can you install NBN at a new home or development?

Yes. We handle NBN installation and lead-in work, and our civils team can complete the trenching and pit and pipe needed before connection.

What is OTDR testing?

OTDR (optical time-domain reflectometer) testing sends light pulses down a fibre to measure loss and pinpoint faults, splices and breaks along the cable. It is used to certify new links and to find faults.

Do you offer on-site assessments?

Yes. For most projects we review the site or the project drawings before providing a written quote.

Do you service my area?

We work across Melbourne and Victoria, with a focus on the western suburbs. Send your project location and we will confirm.

How is pricing worked out?

Pricing depends on the scope, cable lengths, site access and testing requirements, so we confirm it after reviewing the project. You receive a written quote before work starts.
