FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What civil works do you do?

Trenching, telecommunications pit and pipe, underground conduit, excavation, cable hauling support and reinstatement of concrete, asphalt and landscaped surfaces.

Do you do pit and pipe for new developments?

Yes. We install pit and pipe for new estates, townhouse and commercial developments so they are ready for NBN and other network connections.

Do you reinstate surfaces after trenching?

Yes. We restore concrete, asphalt and landscaped areas after works as part of the job.

Do you offer on-site assessments?

Yes. For most projects we review the site or project drawings before providing a written quote.

Do you service my area?

We work across Melbourne and Victoria, with a focus on the western suburbs. Send your project location and we will confirm.

How is pricing worked out?

Pricing depends on trench length and depth, ground conditions, surface types and access, so we confirm it after reviewing the project. You receive a written quote before work starts.

Planning civil or utility works?

Tell us about your project scope, location and timeframe and our team will be in touch.
