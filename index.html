What We Do

Our Services

Engineering, civil execution, and wireline maintenance delivered by specialist field divisions.

Transmission Networks

Deploying high-frequency long-haul backbone routing, swap-link executions, and deep validation testing.

  • Site survey, installation, commissioning, service migration, and legacy equipment removal.
  • Upgrade existing transmission networks with IP, Optics, and Microwave solutions.
  • End-to-end mobile transmission on fibre — PDH.
  • Microwave implementation, testing, and commissioning.
  • PDH, SDH, and DWDM upgrading, protecting, and link swapping.
  • MTX links A–Z including patching, splicing, joints, and routers.
  • IBC/DAS work A–Z: routers, switches, racks, DWDMs, alarm controllers, and air-scale arrays.
  • Comprehensive testing: RFC, OTDR, insertion loss, call tests, PIM testing, and Netgear validation.
  • POC3, TSD, and POC4 parameter execution for Nokia/TPG networks.
  • Line-of-sight (LOS) validation: frequency plans, path profiles, tower height summaries, and path calculations.
  • HOP metrics per MOPs and Australian carrier standards.

Optical Fibre Splicing & Jointing

High-precision fibre integration, multi-core pathways, and critical real-time emergency restorations.

  • Single fusion splicing and precision ribbon splicing.
  • Live fibre cutovers, path migrations, and critical infrastructure rerouting.
  • Live cable works: ringbark and tube splitting to access single fibres safely.
  • Joint building: aerial platforms, FIST's & FOSC's, FTP components, and wall boxes.
  • Fibre joint audits, cutover mapping, and digital recording of complex routing paths.
  • Accredited operations within Sydney Tunnels.
  • Terminating large core counts and high-capacity joint enclosures.
  • Long-distance fibre path construction across transport corridors.
  • FTTX exchange and roadside infrastructure: HDODF, OFDF, FDH, DLM, LM, and HONT installation.
  • As-built documents, DBOR records, MIT matrices, handover records, and fibre workbooks.
  • OTDR testing, insertion loss data, and comprehensive path commissioning reporting.
  • Long-haul network splicing and live field patching.
  • Commissioning and integration of active distribution equipment.
  • ENTU and CPE installation, configuration, and endpoint handover.
  • Node commissioning, NBN MDU engineering, and network surveys.
  • Fault, emergency, and unexpected callout physical remediation.

Wireline Projects

Wireline projects involve deploying and maintaining physical infrastructure — fibre-optic cables and copper wires — to deliver high-speed internet, voice, and data services. These projects are critical to digital transformation and increasing bandwidth demands. Unitech Networks plays a vital role in implementing reliable communication networks that support businesses, homes, and communities.
