FAQs

Solar and battery questions

How much does solar cost in Melbourne?

The price depends on the system size, panel and inverter brands, whether you add a battery, and your roof. After reviewing your bills and property we give you a written quote, so you know the full cost before anything is installed.

What size solar system do I need?

It depends on how much electricity you use, when you use it, and how much roof space you have. We look at your recent power bills to recommend a size that suits your usage rather than simply the biggest system that fits.

Is a battery worth it?

A battery lets you use your daytime solar at night and can provide backup during outages. Whether it pays off depends on your usage, feed-in tariff and system size. We will give you an honest view based on your situation.

Are there government rebates?

Federal and Victorian government rebates and incentives may be available for solar panels and batteries. Eligibility and amounts change from time to time, so we will explain what currently applies to you when we prepare your quote.

Who installs the system?

Installation is carried out by our partner installers, who are licensed electricians with the accreditation required for solar and battery work. Unitech stays your point of contact throughout the project.

Can you add a battery or EV charger to my existing solar?

Yes, in most cases. We check your current inverter and switchboard to confirm compatibility and recommend the simplest way to add a battery or charger.

What warranty do I get?

Panels, inverters and batteries come with manufacturer warranties, and installation workmanship warranty details are included in every quote.
